
## 3rd-person combat heartbeat (2026-10-10, Campton's WoW pacing brief)
- GCD: casters 1.5 s, warrior 1.0 s; haste % (player.state.haste) shortens it to a 0.75 s floor. Was a flat 1.2 s.
- Spell queue: a press in the last 400 ms of the GCD or a cast fires on the exact frame the clock frees (scheduled with ctx.after, not the 0.1 s tick).
- Off-GCD: every kit's keys 9 and 0 are its interrupt (15 s cd, stops a cast, dazes and roots 2 s) and defensive (45 s cd, 50% less damage for 6 s). They work mid-cast.
- Rules: the target has to be in front of you (180° arc) and in line of sight. Bands: 4.5 m melee, 18 m mid, 36 m long casts.
- Moving cancels a cast. Instants never root you.
- Feel: 15% crit for 1.5x damage with a big number, crunch and shake. 20% proc on a single-target hit makes the next Cast-kind spell instant for 10 s; gold wings frame the screen and Cast slots glow.
- A warrior's ability starts his auto-attack swing timer.

- 2026-10-10 (Campton: "scrub the OSRS combat system from the 3rd person mode and separate them (leveling skills, jumping etc) 3rd person is based on talents"): was: class combat fed OSRS skill xp and read skill levels; jump height/stamina came from the jumping/running skills. Now: in a class mode (combatMode ≠ osrs) the player has ONE character level 1–30 (player.state.tp.xp; 4 xp per damage, 25 per kill, xpToNext = 60·lv^1.5) and one talent point per level from 2, spent on an 11-talent, 4-tier tree (tiers open at 0/5/10/15 spent) in scripts/lib/data/talents.js. Spell power = character level, health = 10 + 3/level (+Fortitude), jump is a plain full jump, no stamina drain. OSRS skills, xp, stamina and skill-gated jumping are untouched in OSRS mode. N opens the talent tree in 3rd person (OSRS spellbook stays on N in OSRS).
