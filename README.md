# RealmCrafter ⚔️ 🌲

> **Build. Script. Play.**  
> A modular 3D world-building toolkit and RPG engine designed for rapid asset staging, terrain calibration, and classic tab-target MMO mechanics.

---

### Key Features

* **Asset Palette Testbed:** Dedicated staging lanes and radial biome pods for rapid scale checking, LOD validation, and seam inspection.
* **Terrain & Splat Mapping:** Real-time multi-texture brush blending, ground foliage scatter, and terrain-to-mesh transitions.
* **Scale & Clearance Bay:** Built-in humanoid and mount calibration bounds to verify doorways, camera occlusion, and collision geometry instantly.
* **Modular Staging Rows:** Aligned snap grids for architectural sets, vegetation height racks, and prop clutter libraries.
* **Classic RPG Systems:** Pre-configured tab-targeting framework, ability/spell cooldown logic, and character clearance bounds.
